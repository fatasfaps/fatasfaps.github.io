Hi there, I'm Fatima —or, Fatas, the name I've gone with for the longest time! 

I'm a fresh graduate in Biomedical Engineering from [Airlangga University](https://fst.unair.ac.id/s1-teknik-biomedis/), where I was particularly invested in the research on incorporating AI into medical diagnosis and treatment during my studies. Beyond that, I'm a believer of conventional to personalized medicine transformation —one in which AI serves as a vital catalyst to bring this future to life. For the most part, I find myself gravitating towards dissecting the dynamics of human beings and their brains, encompassing the complexities of how they function at the cognitive, physiological, and behavioral levels. Hence, my deep curiosity in neuroscience and its evolving potential to shape the future of science, technology, and their integration into meaningful human-centered systems. 

As I'm about to embark on a new phase in life, I've been navigating this transition by catching up on some [learning](https://fatasfaps.github.io/logs/) as well as working on independent [projects](https://fatasfaps.github.io/projects/). Outside of the sufficient deduction required to tackle those two, I tend to linger in thought about the things I observe and sense on a daily basis. Some of them fascinate me so irresistibly that I've been trying to write them down —kindly check my [pieces](https://medium.com/@fatasfaps) and [scribbles](https://fatasfaps.github.io/scribbles/) to find out more. 

I typically love to spend my time [reading](https://fatasfaps.github.io/books/) and watching [movies](https://fatasfaps.github.io/movies/). Additionally, I've always been keen on making music and engaging in creative activities —such as painting, cooking, and crafting. For the physical part, I'm an average exerciser: a Sunday town square runner (or walker, to be fair) and a casual yogi —only when I have enough macro intake, some plucky good attitude, and a slight can-do spirit that I take up my sister's invitation to play tennis :D

It's a pleasure to welcome you to my page. If anything, feel free to reach out to me through [email](mailto:fatimahasyapk2015@gmail.com) —I'm always down for collaborations and nice conversations about science, tech, life, and anything in between!
